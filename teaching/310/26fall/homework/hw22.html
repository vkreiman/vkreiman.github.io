Homework 22: Correlation

  1. Section 4.6, #9, 10.
  2. Suppose that \(X\) and \(Y\) have joint pdf \begin{equation} f_{X,Y}(x,y)= \begin{cases} x+y, & \text{if }0\leq x\leq 1\text{ and } 0\leq y\leq 1\\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \end{equation} Find Cov\((X,Y)\) and Corr\((X,Y)\).
