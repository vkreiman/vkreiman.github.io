## Homework 11: The Cumulative Distribution Function

1. Section 3.3, #2.

2. Let \(X\) be the lifetime of a certain electronic device (measured in hours). Suppose that the probability density function of \(X\) is given by 

   $$ f_X(x)=
   \begin{cases}
   \frac{10}{x^2}, & x \gt 10\\
   0, & x\leq 10
   \end{cases}
   $$

   1. Find and sketch \(F_X(x)\).
   2. Find \(P(X \gt 20)\) using the cdf.
   {: .nested-list}

3. A continuous random variable \(X\) has pdf given by 

   $$
   f_X(x)=
   \begin{cases}
   1 - |x|, &|x|\leq 1\\
   0, & |x| \gt 1
   \end{cases}
   $$

   1. Find and sketch \(F_X(x)\).
   2. Find \(P(X \lt 1.5)\) using the cdf.
   {: .nested-list}
    
4. A continuous random variable \(X\) has cdf given by

   $$
   F_X(x)= \begin{cases} 0, & x\leq 0\\
   \frac{1}{9}x^2, & 0 \lt x\leq 3\\
   1, & x\gt 3 \end{cases}
   $$
   
   1. Find and sketch \(f_X(x)\).
   2. Find \(P(1 \lt X\leq 2)\) using the cdf.
   {: .nested-list}
