Homework 22: Hypergeometric, Geometric, and
Negative Binomial Distributions

  1. To demonstrate his benevolence, Darth Vader decides to use the Death Star to randomly destroy six of the twelve planets of the Dagobah system (rather than all twelve, as suggested by the Emperor). Four of the twelve planets harbor life.
    1. Find the probability that at least three of the four planets harboring life are destroyed.
    2. Find the expected number of planets harboring life that will be destroyed.
    For both parts, you should ignore the fact that Yoda lives in the Dagobah system.
  2. Because of his past convictions for fraud, Greeedo has a 20% chance each year of having his tax returns audited. What is the probability that he will not be audited for the next three years?
  3. Han Solo is applying for a pilot certificate. Assume that his probability of passing the flying test on any attempt is \(0.20\). Let \(X\) be the number of tries that he needs in order to pass the flying test.
    1. Find \(f_X(x)\).
    2. Find \(y\) such that the probability of Han not passing the test after taking it \(y\) times is under 5%.
  4. The Millenium Falcon can withstand four direct hits from an enemy destroyer, but no more. Suppose that an Imperial Navy Destroyer fires a barrage of missiles at the Falcon, each with a 20% chance of hitting. Find the probability that the twelfth missile destroys the Falcon.
  5. The rebel forces fire ten missiles at the death star. Six of the ten are precision strike missiles. To intercept the missiles, the death star launches seven antiballistic missiles, each of which will destroy exactly one of the incoming rockets. The antiballistic missiles are unable to detect which of the ten are precision strike rockets.
    1. Find the probability that the death star will be hit by at least two precision strike rockets.
    2. Find the expected number of precision strike rockets that will hit the death star.
  6. Let \(X\) be the number of Bernoulli trials required to produce at least one success and at least one failure.
    1. Find a formula for \(f_X(x)\).
    2. Find \(E(X)\).
