--- layout: course title: Calculus I number: Math 221 semester: Spring 2026 ---
This is a course on introductory differential and integral calculus and their applications. Topics include limits, continuity, differentiation, applications of the derivative, rate of change, integration, applications of the integral, transcendental functions, and inverse functions.
